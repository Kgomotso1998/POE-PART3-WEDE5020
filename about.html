Our Story

Operational since 2021, Artisan Crust is a family-owned neighborhood bakery specializing in handcrafted sourdough bread, artisanal pastries, and custom event cakes.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To craft fresh, organic baked goods using traditional fermentation techniques and locally sourced ingredients.

Our Vision

To become the region's preferred choice for premium artisanal baked goods and custom celebration catering.

Meet Our Team

  • Lead Baker: Expert in sourdough starter cultivation and long-fermentation doughs.
  • Head Pastry Chef: Specialist in French viennoiserie and custom cake decorating.
